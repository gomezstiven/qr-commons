# ADR-0001 — Static authoring is the project boundary

Status: **Accepted**  
Date: **2026-09-13**

## Context

QR products often combine two technically different capabilities: generating the QR artifact and operating infrastructure behind the encoded destination. Combining them creates unnecessary lock-in and makes a static artifact depend on a vendor service.

## Decision

QR Commons owns static QR authoring only:

`payload -> normalize -> encode -> render -> validate -> export`

Managed redirects, mutable destinations, scan analytics, campaign management, accounts, billing, and hosted lifecycle services are outside the project boundary.

A static QR generated by QR Commons must not silently replace a user-provided destination with a QR Commons URL.

## Consequences

- the project can be used and self-hosted without a QR Commons backend;
- generated artifacts remain useful independently of the project;
- commercial dynamic-QR products can exist above or beside QR Commons without forcing commercial concerns into the static core;
- implementation packages should remain reusable outside the reference web app.
